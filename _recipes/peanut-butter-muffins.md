<div class="lang" data-lang="en">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">Peanut Butter Muffins</h1>

  <div class="container">
    <p><strong>Yield:</strong> 6–8 muffins</p>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">Ingredients</h2>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Peanut butter</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">80 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Egg (large)</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Milk</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">120 mL</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Vanilla</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">½ tsp</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Baking powder</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">¾ tsp</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Flour</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">96 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Salt</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">¼ tsp</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Sugar</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">70 g</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Preheat the oven to 180°C and prepare 6–8 muffin cups.</li>
      <li>Mix the peanut butter, egg, milk, vanilla, and sugar together.</li>
      <li>Combine the flour, baking powder, and salt, then mix into the wet ingredients just until combined.</li>
      <li>Divide the batter among the muffin cups. Bake at 180°C for 20–30 minutes.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Notes</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Omit the salt if the peanut butter is already salted.</li>
      <li>If using unsweetened peanut butter, you can increase the sugar.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lang" data-lang="ja">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">ピーナッツバターマフィン</h1>

  <div class="container">
    <p><strong>分量：</strong> 6〜8個</p>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">材料</h2>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">ピーナッツバター</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">80 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">卵（Lサイズ）</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1個</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">牛乳</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">120 mL</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">バニラ</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">小さじ½</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">ベーキングパウダー</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">小さじ¾</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">小麦粉</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">96 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">塩</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">小さじ¼</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">砂糖</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">70 g</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">作り方</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>オーブンを180℃に予熱し、マフィン型を6〜8個用意する。</li>
      <li>ピーナッツバター、卵、牛乳、バニラ、砂糖を混ぜ合わせる。</li>
      <li>小麦粉、ベーキングパウダー、塩を合わせ、液体の材料に加えて粉気がなくなるまで混ぜる。</li>
      <li>生地を型に分け入れ、180℃で20〜30分焼く。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">メモ</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>塩入りのピーナッツバターを使う場合、塩は不要。</li>
      <li>無糖のピーナッツバターを使う場合、砂糖を増やしてもよい。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>
