<div class="lang" data-lang="en">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">🐙 Octopus Rice</h1>
  <div class="container">
    <p><strong>Yield:</strong> Serves 4</p>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">Ingredients</h2>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Japanese short-grain rice</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2 gō (360mL uncooked)</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Water</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">To the rice cooker’s 2-gō line, after adding seasonings</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Boiled octopus</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">150g</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Cooked, shelled edamame</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">50g</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Ginger</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">Amount not specified</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Soy sauce</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2 tbsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Sake</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 tbsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Hondashi (dashi granules)</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2 heaped tsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Salt</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1/2 tsp</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Wash the rice and soak for at least 30 minutes (soaking time is excluded from the cooking time). Drain in a sieve.</li>
      <li>Cut the octopus into 1.5cm pieces. Julienne the ginger.</li>
      <li>Put the drained rice, soy sauce, sake, Hondashi, and salt into the rice cooker. Add water to the 2-gō line. Top with the octopus and edamame, then cook.</li>
      <li>Once cooked, add the ginger and mix gently.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>
