<div class="lang" data-lang="en">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">🍫 Rich Chocolate Cake (Nama Chocolate Style)</h1>
  <p>Makes 1 loaf-pan cake.</p>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">Ingredients</h2>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Bittersweet chocolate</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">200g</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Butter</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">60g</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Sugar</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 tbsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Heavy cream</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2 tbsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Wheat flour</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">3 tbsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Eggs</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Powdered sugar (optional topping)</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">As desired</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Break the chocolate into pieces. Melt it with the butter and sugar in a bowl over a hot-water bath.</li>
      <li>Add the heavy cream, flour, and eggs in that order, mixing after each addition.</li>
      <li>Pour into a loaf pan and tap gently to release air bubbles. Bake at 180°C for 30 minutes. The cake is ready when a toothpick inserted into it comes out without batter sticking to it.</li>
    </ol>
    <p>Optional: Cool the cake and dust with powdered sugar.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lang" data-lang="ja">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">🍫 生チョコ風 濃厚チョコケーキ</h1>
  <p>パウンドケーキ型1個分。</p>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">材料</h2>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">板チョコ（ビター）</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">200g</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">バター</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">60g</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">砂糖</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">大さじ1</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">生クリーム</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">大さじ2</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">小麦粉</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">大さじ3</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">卵</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2個</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">粉砂糖（お好みで）</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">適量</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">作り方</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>板チョコを砕いて、バターと砂糖と一緒に湯せんしながら溶かす。</li>
      <li>生クリーム、小麦粉、卵の順で加えて混ぜる。</li>
      <li>空気を抜くようにパウンドケーキ型に入れ、180℃で30分焼く。楊枝に生地がつかなければOK。</li>
    </ol>
    <p>お好みで、冷やしてから粉砂糖をかけてもよい。</p>
  </div>
</div>
