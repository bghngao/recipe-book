    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Raw nuts and/or seeds</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">225 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Fine-grain sea salt</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 tsp</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Ground cinnamon</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">½ tsp</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Coconut oil or olive oil</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">60 ml</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Maple syrup or honey</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">120 ml</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Vanilla extract</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 tsp</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Dried fruit (optional)</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">To taste</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Chocolate chips (optional)</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">To taste</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Coconut flakes (optional)</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">To taste</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Preheat the oven to 170°C.</li>
      <li>In a bowl, mix the oats, nuts and/or seeds, salt, and cinnamon. Add the oil, maple syrup or honey, and vanilla extract, then mix well.</li>
      <li>Spread the mixture on a baking sheet and bake for 21–24 minutes.</li>
      <li>Let cool for at least 45 minutes. Add any optional dried fruit, chocolate chips, or coconut flakes as desired.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Notes</h2>
    <p>Store at room temperature for 1–2 weeks or freeze for up to 3 months.</p>
  </div>
</div>
