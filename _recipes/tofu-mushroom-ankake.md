<div class="lang" data-lang="en">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">🍄 Tofu with Mushroom Ankake</h1>
  <div class="container">
    <p><strong>Yield:</strong> Serves 1–2</p>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">Ingredients</h2>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Tofu</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">150g</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Shimeji mushrooms</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1/2 pack</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Straight (ready-to-use) mentsuyu</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">80mL</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Sesame oil</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">As needed</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Potato starch</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 tsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Water (for the starch slurry)</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2 tsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Ginger paste (from a tube)</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">A little</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Stir-fry the shimeji mushrooms in sesame oil.</li>
      <li>Add the mentsuyu and ginger paste, then stir to combine.</li>
      <li>Mix the potato starch with the water to make a slurry. Reduce the heat to low before adding it, stir until the sauce thickens, then turn off the heat.</li>
      <li>Cover the tofu with plastic wrap and microwave at 600W for 1 minute. Pour the mushroom sauce over the tofu and serve.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>

    <h2 class="title-instruction">作り方</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>ごま油でしめじを炒める。</li>
      <li>麺つゆとしょうがチューブを入れ、混ぜる。</li>
      <li>片栗粉を水で溶く。弱火にしてから水溶き片栗粉を入れ、混ぜてとろみがついたら火を消す。</li>
      <li>豆腐にラップをして600Wの電子レンジで1分加熱する。きのこあんをかけて完成。</li>
    </ol>
