<div class="lang" data-lang="en">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">🥣 Korean-Style Scallion, Nori & Tofu Soup</h1>
  <div class="container">
    <p><strong>Yield:</strong> Serves 2</p>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">Ingredients</h2>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Silken tofu</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 small block</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Scallion</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 stalk</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Seasoned nori</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 packet</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Chicken stock powder</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 tsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Soy sauce</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2 tsp</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Sesame oil</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">A little</div>
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Water</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">400mL</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Thinly slice the scallion into rounds. Cut the tofu into 1cm cubes and place it in a pot.</li>
      <li>Add the water and chicken stock powder. Tear the nori into small pieces and add it to the pot, then heat over medium heat.</li>
      <li>Once the soup comes to a boil, reduce the heat to low. Add the scallion and soy sauce, then add a little sesame oil.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>
