<div class="lang" data-lang="en">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">Best Classic Italian Tiramisu</h1>

  <div class="container">
    <p>8–10 servings</p>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">Ingredients</h2>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Mascarpone cheese, cold from the fridge</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">400 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Egg yolks</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">4</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Granulated sugar</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">100–110 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Vanilla</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">1 tsp</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Salt</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">¼ tsp</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Heavy cream</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">300 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Ladyfingers</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">30–36</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Strong black coffee, room temperature</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">200 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">Cocoa powder</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">2 tbsp</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Beat the cold mascarpone at medium speed for 30–60 seconds.</li>
      <li>In a separate bowl, beat the egg yolks and granulated sugar at medium-high speed for about 2 minutes, until pale and fluffy. Do not overmix.</li>
      <li>Add the egg yolk mixture to the mascarpone, then add the salt and vanilla. Mix at medium speed just until combined, then use a rubber spatula to mix from the bottom of the bowl.</li>
      <li>In another bowl, whip the heavy cream until stiff peaks form. If it is underwhipped, the tiramisu will not hold its shape.</li>
      <li>Gently fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture with a rubber spatula, taking care not to deflate it.</li>
      <li>Quickly dip both sides of the ladyfingers into the room-temperature coffee and arrange a layer in a serving dish.</li>
      <li>Spread half of the cream mixture over the ladyfingers.</li>
      <li>Add another layer of ladyfingers quickly dipped in coffee on both sides.</li>
      <li>Spread the remaining cream mixture on top.</li>
      <li>Refrigerate overnight.</li>
      <li>Dust with cocoa powder just before serving.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lang" data-lang="ja">
  <h1 class="title-recipe">本格イタリアンティラミス</h1>

  <div class="container">
    <p>8〜10人分</p>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-ingredient">材料</h2>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">マスカルポーネチーズ（冷蔵庫から出した冷たいもの）</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">400 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">卵黄</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">4個</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">グラニュー糖</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">100〜110 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">バニラ</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">小さじ1</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">塩</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">小さじ¼</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">生クリーム</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">300 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">レディフィンガー</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">30〜36本</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">濃いブラックコーヒー（常温）</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">200 g</div>
    </div>

    <div class="ingredient-quantity">
      <div class="column-left-ingredient">ココアパウダー</div>
      <div class="column-right-amount">大さじ2</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="title-instruction">作り方</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>冷たいマスカルポーネを中速で30〜60秒混ぜる。</li>
      <li>別のボウルに卵黄とグラニュー糖を入れ、中高速で2分ほど混ぜる。白っぽくふわふわになったら止め、混ぜすぎないこと。</li>
      <li>卵黄の生地をマスカルポーネに加え、塩とバニラを入れる。中速で混ざるまで混ぜ、ボウルの底からゴムベラで混ぜる。</li>
      <li>別のボウルで生クリームを角が立つまで泡立てる。泡立てが足りないとティラミスが形を保てないので注意する。</li>
      <li>泡立てた生クリームをマスカルポーネの生地に加え、泡をつぶさないようにゴムベラで優しく混ぜる。</li>
      <li>レディフィンガーの両面を常温のコーヒーにさっと浸し、器に並べる。</li>
      <li>クリームの半量を広げる。</li>
      <li>レディフィンガーの両面をコーヒーにさっと浸し、もう一段並べる。</li>
      <li>残りのクリームをのせて広げる。</li>
      <li>冷蔵庫で一晩冷やす。</li>
      <li>食べる直前にココアパウダーをかける。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>
